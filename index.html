About

I work in fluid mechanics and computational mechanics at the University of Cádiz, in the research group TEP-966 Mathematical Engineering. My interests lie where physical models, numerical simulation and data meet engineering and biological problems.

Research

Current lines of work:

  • Low-Reynolds-number locomotion and active matter. Swimming at small scales, where viscous forces dominate and motion has no inertia; collective behaviour of active particles in Stokes flow.
  • Mechanics of soft biological tissues. Constitutive characterisation of tissue from mechanical tests and imaging: abdominal aortic aneurysms (4D ultrasound strain imaging) and growth-plate cartilage under compression and shear.
  • Transport and deposition in biomass boilers. CFD and experiments on the deposition of ash particles on boiler surfaces, with Miguel Díaz Troyano and Chungen Yin.
  • Modelling of manufacturing processes. Superplastic forming and dimensional analysis of free-bulge tests, machining of light alloys, surface quality and additive manufacturing.
  • Earlier work: nonlinear data analysis. Nonlinear dimensionality reduction applied to climate time series (El Niño and the annual cycle).
